Local creator marketing: evidence-based guides

We publish what the research actually shows about small-creator marketing, not what the industry deck says. Every claim here traces to a named source: peer-reviewed journals where they exist, large surveys with published methodology where they don't, and vendor statistics labelled as vendor statistics.

The focus is narrow on purpose. Nano and micro creators, local audiences, barter collaborations, and the practical question a café owner in Kallio actually has: which creator, what do I offer, and how do I know it worked. Finnish disclosure rules under the KKV 2025 guidance are covered where they apply.
