Frequently asked questions

How many followers do I need?

500 is enough. We rank creators by engagement rate and audience quality, not size — a 900-follower account with loyal local readers is more valuable to a neighbourhood café than a 50k account with a scattered audience.

Does it really cost nothing?

Nothing, ever. Creators browse, apply and connect for free. Businesses pay for platform features — never a commission on your deals.

What kind of deals can I get?

Whatever local businesses offer: restaurant dinners, haircuts and beauty treatments, fitness passes, products, event tickets, experiences. Every offer states up front what you get and what content is expected.

Do I have to mark the content as an ad?

Yes — follow your platform's branded-content rules and local advertising-disclosure laws (e.g. tag it as a paid partnership or #ad). Honest disclosure protects you and keeps your audience's trust.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X and Threads. You choose which platform and format each collab uses — reels, stories, posts or reviews.
