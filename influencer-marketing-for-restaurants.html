Why restaurants use barter collabs

A restaurant is an experience, so the best ad for it is someone eating there. Local food creators show the room, the plate and the queue to people who can come tonight.

Guests from your area

Creator cards show where the audience is. Pick people whose followers live in Helsinki, Espoo or Vantaa, not abroad.

Food cost, not media budget

A dinner for two costs you its food cost. There is no agency fee and no commission on the collab on paid plans.

Fill the slow days

Book creator visits for Monday to Wednesday evenings or a new lunch menu, when an extra table matters most.

Content you can reuse

Ask the creator to tag you and agree whether you may repost the video on your own account.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The campaign costs what your offer costs you. New businesses get 14 days of Growth free. After that: (almost) Free is €15 once for one offer and three contact unlocks, refunded in one click if the offer gets fewer than three applications in 14 days. Starter is €69 a month and Growth €109 a month. No commission on collaborations while you are on a paid plan.

Do restaurant creators really work for a meal?

Many nano and micro creators do, especially for a new place or a new menu. Larger creators with tens of thousands of followers usually also ask for a fee. You see who applies to your offer before you pay for more.

Which creators should a restaurant pick?

Pick by local audience, not follower count. A creator with 2,000 followers who mostly live in Helsinki brings more possible guests than one with 50,000 spread around the world.

Can I serve alcohol in the offer?

You can include drinks, but alcohol advertising in Finland has its own rules. Keep alcohol out of the posts or check the Alcohol Act guidance before asking for it.
