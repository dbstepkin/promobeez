Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The campaign itself costs whatever your barter offer is worth to you — a pizza, a haircut, a gym pass. Promobeez has a free tier (1 active offer, 1 contact unlock) and paid plans that unlock more offers and contacts. We never take a commission on deals.

How do I know the audience is really local?

Creator cards show audience geography based on follower data — the percentage of a creator's audience located in your city. That's the metric that matters for a local business, and it's front and centre.

What kind of creators are on Promobeez?

Nano and micro creators from 500 followers: food bloggers, lifestyle creators, fitness enthusiasts — people whose recommendations their neighbours actually trust. Small audiences, high engagement.

Does Promobeez manage the collaboration?

No — and that's by design. Promobeez is an introduction service: we match and connect you, then you agree on the details directly with the creator. No middlemen slowing things down or taking a cut.

What if a creator doesn't deliver?

You unlock contacts one at a time and start small, so the risk per collab is the cost of one pizza — not a media budget. Reputation signals in creator profiles help you pick reliable partners.
