Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The campaign itself costs whatever your barter offer is worth to you — a pizza, a haircut, a gym pass. New businesses get 14 days of Growth free. After that: (almost) Free (€15 once for 1 offer and 3 contact unlocks, refunded in one click if the offer gets fewer than 3 applications in 14 days), Starter at €69/mo, or Growth at €109/mo.Basic is €15 a month and includes your first offer: 5 applications, all of them unlockable. After that you add application packs per offer and pay only for applications that arrive: 5 for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift.

How do I know the audience is really local?

Each creator card shows the city the creator is based in and the share of their audience in your city. That's the number that matters for a local business, and it's front and centre.

What kind of creators are on Promobeez?

Nano and micro creators from 500 followers: food bloggers, lifestyle creators, fitness enthusiasts — people whose recommendations their neighbours actually trust. Small audiences, high engagement.

Does Promobeez manage the collaboration?

You agree on the details directly with the creator, and nobody takes a cut. Promobeez handles the rest: a calendar for visits, a WhatsApp reminder to the creator before the visit, and a dashboard that ticks off the agreed posts once they're published.

What if a creator doesn't deliver?

You start small, so the risk per collab is the cost of one meal, not a media budget. If a creator goes quiet, tap Report a problem and we nudge them by email and WhatsApp. If an agreed post is taken down, you get an email. Reviews from other businesses help you pick people who deliver.

Can I check a creator who messaged me directly?

Yes. Paste their profile link or @username into Check a creator and you get the same card you see for applicants: score, local audience, engagement and recent brand deals. Every paid plan includes 5 checks a month.
