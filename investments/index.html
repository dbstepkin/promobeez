Business model

Venues pay a subscription and buy creator unlocks. Creators join free.

No cut of the creator deal: the venue pays us, the creator gets the experience.

We are still experimenting with pricing. These prices are not final.

How small venues buy

Customers pulse. They don't churn.

A café buys a month, pauses, comes back for the next season. Each band is one month's new venues; older bands keep coming back.

Market

Marketing money is moving to creators

And small venues, the biggest group of businesses, have been left out of it.

Competition
