What's new

Creator collabs, now with a guest count

Collabs with local creators work for restaurants. Their followers live in your area and trust them, and you get photos and videos you can reuse. The one thing you couldn't see so far is how many people walked in because of a post. This pilot adds that number.

What creator collabs give you

  • People nearby hear about you from someone they follow
  • Real photos and videos of your food and your room
  • You pay with a meal, not with a media budget
  • Posts that people keep finding weeks after they go live

What the pilot adds on top

  • The creator earns for each guest who actually comes in
  • So the creator keeps inviting people after the first post
  • Every visit is confirmed at the table by your waiter
  • You get a plain count: who came, which day, from which creator
How it works

Four steps, no app, nothing to install

Your guests don't download anything and your staff don't learn new software. Your waiters only need to know one secret code.

1

A creator posts about you

A reel or a post about your place, with your welcome offer, the creator's personal code and the link.

2

The guest saves it

One tap on save in Instagram or TikTok. Nothing to install, nothing to remember.

3

The waiter confirms

At the table the guest opens the coupon page from the link in the saved post. The waiter enters your 4-digit secret code and the table number. About ten seconds.

4

You see the count

The guest gets the gift, the visit is counted, the creator earns their fee. You see every guest by date and by creator.

Why it's worth your time

What a pilot restaurant gets

You get a month of Growth on us, creators who want to visit, and a clear count of how many guests each one brought. We get your feedback.

A number you can trust

Each visit is confirmed inside your restaurant by your own staff. If a guest didn't sit at your table, it doesn't count.

A month of Growth on us

The Growth plan (€109 a month) is yours for the first month at no cost. Your offer goes out to local creators on Promobeez and you see everyone who applies.

Creators who want to fill seats

When a creator earns per guest, they talk about you more than once, answer their followers' questions and tag you again on a slow week.

You set the rules

You pick the gift and the days it's valid. Want new faces on Tuesday and Wednesday evenings and not on full Saturdays? Say so and that's how we set it up.

Almost no work for your team

No app for guests, no app for staff, no link to your till. Your waiters only need the venue's 4-digit secret code.

You shape the product

Pilot restaurants talk to the founders directly. If something is awkward for your staff or your guests, we change it, usually within days.

Questions

Before you apply

Does the pilot cost anything?

The Growth plan is free for the first month (normally €109 a month). For each confirmed guest you pay a fee through Promobeez, which we agree on together on the first call. We pass it on to the creator and handle the payout and taxes. You also give that guest the welcome offer you chose. A post that brings no one costs you nothing.

What if guests only come for the gift?

That's one of the things the pilot is meant to measure. You set the conditions for the gift yourself: for example, a minimum bill, the days and hours it's valid, or one gift per table. So the cost stays under your control, and you'll see what those guests ordered on top.

What does my staff need to do?

When a guest opens the coupon page from the link in the post, the waiter enters the venue's 4-digit secret code and the table number on the guest's phone.

Which creators will post about us?

Local nano and micro creators whose followers are mostly in Helsinki. You see who they are before anything goes live, and you can say no to anyone.

What happens to guests' phone numbers?

The number is used only to confirm a visit once, so the same person isn't counted twice. We don't share guests' numbers with restaurants.

How long does the pilot run?

We agree on the dates with you on the first call, so it fits your calendar and avoids your busiest weeks.
