# CastersApp alternative for local venues: Promobeez vs CastersApp compared

By [Andrey Shepelev](https://www.promobeez.com/about/andrey-shepelev) · Published 22 Oct 2026 · Facts checked 22 Oct 2026 · 7 min read

> **Short answer.** CastersApp, from the Finnish agency Casters, is a managed creator marketplace: you book a demo, a campaign manager proposes creators, both you and Casters approve every post, and all payments run through the platform. It is strong on UGC videos for your ads and on paid influencer posts. Prices for brands are not published. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace for local venues: you post an offer such as a dinner or a treatment, nearby nano and micro creators apply, you pay €15 a month and buy extra applications only when you want more creators, and the creator relationship is yours to keep. Pick CastersApp if you want UGC for ads or a managed paid campaign. Pick Promobeez if you want steady local visits for the cost of what you already sell.

## 2. Looking for a CastersApp alternative? {#alternative}

Local businesses that search for a CastersApp alternative usually want one of three things CastersApp does not offer by design. They want a price before a demo: CastersApp publishes no business prices.[[2]](#ref-2) They want to keep the creators: CastersApp's terms stop a creator from working with you directly for 12 months after you meet on the platform.[[5]](#ref-5) And they want barter without a cash fee on top: most open CastersApp campaigns combine money with a gift, such as €100 plus a €100 gift card and a meal for two.[[3]](#ref-3)

Promobeez is the CastersApp alternative for that case: a public €15 plan, barter only, and the creator contact is yours from the moment you unlock it. If you need UGC videos for your ads or a campaign manager to run everything, CastersApp is the better fit.

## 3. How CastersApp works {#casters}

Casters started as an influencer agency in Helsinki and built CastersApp as its platform. The business page says the team handles "creators, briefing, coordination and reporting" for you, and the homepage promises each brand its own campaign manager from start to finish.[[1]](#ref-1)[[2]](#ref-2)

The process has five steps: you state the goal, Casters suggests creators, you approve them, the creators produce the content, and you get a report.[[2]](#ref-2) Creators see open campaigns in the app and apply. On 22 October 2026 there were 21 open campaigns, across four formats:[[3]](#ref-3)

- **UGC videos** that the brand posts on its own Meta and TikTok accounts. Example: a supplement brand paying €170 per selected video plus €20 per extra hook.
- **Influencer posts** on the creator's own account. Example: a Helsinki restaurant offering €100, a €100 gift card and a meal for two for one Reel and three Stories.
- **Barter.** Example: a Christmas party event offering tickets for the creator and friends in return for a pre-event post and Stories.
- **Events and product seeding.**

Creators join free, need more than 1,000 followers, and see the reward before they apply.[[4]](#ref-4) The site gives two sets of totals: 2,000+ influencers, 50+ brands and 150+ campaigns on the homepage, and 1,000+ creators, 100+ Finnish brands and 500+ campaigns on the business page.[[1]](#ref-1)[[2]](#ref-2)

CastersApp lists restaurants as a target group and shows a restaurant launch case with 10 food creators, 40+ pieces of content and table bookings up 200%. The restaurant is not named.[[2]](#ref-2)

## 4. How Promobeez works {#promobeez}

Promobeez is a barter marketplace. A business posts an offer, for example "dinner for two for a Reel and two Stories". Creators with a local audience apply. Before you pay anything, you see each applicant's engagement, how much of their audience is local, their content profile and how recently they did commercial posts. Identity stays hidden until you unlock.

You unlock the contacts you like and agree the visit directly. After the visit, Promobeez watches the published post so you can see that the barter was honoured. We hold no money. Creators never pay.[[6]](#ref-6)

## 5. CastersApp prices vs Promobeez: what a restaurant collab costs {#cost}

CastersApp does not publish business pricing. Its business FAQ calls pricing "transparent", but the pricing link leads to the FAQ, not to a price list, and the main call to action is "book a demo".[[2]](#ref-2) What we can see is the creator side. In the restaurant campaign above, each creator receives €100 in cash, a €100 gift card and a meal for two.[[3]](#ref-3) Casters' own FAQ puts micro-influencers (1,000 to 10,000 followers) at €50 to €500 per post.[[2]](#ref-2) Whatever Casters charges the brand comes on top.

On Promobeez the cost is the plan plus your offer:[[6]](#ref-6)

- **Basic, €15/month:** offers, creator cards, CRM, meeting calendar and post tracking, 5 creator checks a month. No fee on collaborations while you are subscribed.
- **Welcome bonus:** your first offer includes 5 applications, and you can contact all five.
- **Application packs:** 5 applications for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift. You pay only for applications that arrive.

If a dinner for two costs you €25 in food, that is the campaign. A cash fee does buy more control: fixed deliverables, deadlines and, on CastersApp, a formal approval step and usage rights.

## 6. The rules creators sign {#rules}

The CastersApp Terms of Service (updated January 2026) set strict rules for creators, and they shape what you get as a brand:[[5]](#ref-5)

- Nothing is posted until **both the brand and Casters approve** it, and creators must revise until both are satisfied, with no cap on rounds.
- Posts must **stay live for six months**. No other ads 12 hours before or after, and no direct competitor for one week.
- The brand may repost the content on its own organic channels for the campaign plus six months. **Using it in paid ads needs a separate content licence fee.**
- **Payments are held by CastersApp** and released after the post is verified and a 14-day window.
- **Non-circumvention:** for 12 months after first contact, a creator may not work directly with a brand met through CastersApp. Breaking this closes the account and forfeits pending pay.

For a brand that wants control, these rules are a plus. For a café that wants the same three local creators back every month on its own terms, the non-circumvention clause matters: the relationship stays on the platform for a year.

Promobeez works the other way round. Once you unlock a contact, you can invite that creator back whenever you like, directly.

## 7. When CastersApp is the better choice {#choose-casters}

- **You want UGC videos for your own ads**, with formal usage rights. Promobeez does not sell UGC.
- **You want someone to run it**: a campaign manager, a creator shortlist and a report.
- **You pay creators cash** and want contracts, approvals and payouts handled.
- **You need content from outside your neighbourhood**, or a campaign for an event, a car or a cruise.
- **You want a formal approval step** before anything goes live.

## 8. When Promobeez is the better choice {#choose-promobeez}

- **You are a local venue** (café, restaurant, salon, gym, studio) and want people from your area to visit.
- **You want a known price** and can start at €15 a month, with 5 applications included in your first offer.
- **You would rather give a meal than pay a fee.**
- **You want to choose creators yourself**, by local audience, not from a shortlist.
- **You want to keep the creator contact** and work with good creators again without a middleman.

## 9. Switching from CastersApp, or using both {#switch}

CastersApp and Promobeez solve different jobs, so many venues could use both: CastersApp for UGC videos that run as ads, Promobeez for steady local visits. If you move your local collaborations over, keep three things in mind.

- **Respect the 12-month rule.** Creators you met on CastersApp may not work with you directly for 12 months after first contact, so meet new local creators on Promobeez instead of re-inviting them outside the platform.[[5]](#ref-5)
- **Reuse your offer.** The meal, ticket or treatment from your CastersApp campaign becomes your Promobeez offer, without the cash fee.
- **Agree usage rights directly.** On Promobeez there is no platform licence; if you want to reuse a video in ads, agree it with the creator.

Start with [Promobeez Basic for €15 a month](https://www.promobeez.com/for-businesses) and see which local creators apply.

## 11. FAQ {#faq}

### What is CastersApp?

A Finnish creator marketplace run by the influencer agency Casters. Brands get UGC videos, influencer posts, barter and event campaigns with a campaign manager. Creators join free from 1,000 followers.[[1]](#ref-1)[[4]](#ref-4)

### How much does CastersApp cost for a business?

Business prices are not published; you book a demo. On the creator side, open campaigns pay from about €100 per post or video plus gifts, and Casters' FAQ quotes €50 to €500 per post for micro-influencers.[[2]](#ref-2)[[3]](#ref-3)

### How much does Promobeez cost?

Basic is €15 a month, and your first offer includes 5 applications you can all contact. Extra applications come in packs: 5 for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift. You pay only for applications that arrive.[[6]](#ref-6)

### What is the best CastersApp alternative for a local business?

For a café, restaurant, salon or gym that wants local visits for barter and wants to keep the creator contacts, Promobeez is the closest alternative, from €15 a month. For paid campaigns on a public price list, Vaiku starts at €149 a month. For an agency that also runs PR events, look at Invited Agency.

### What do reviews say about CastersApp?

We found no Trustpilot page for CastersApp as of November 2026, and its app store ratings were not readable when we checked. Casters shows a restaurant launch case with table bookings up 200%, but does not name the restaurant. Ask for references from venues like yours.

### Can I work with a creator directly after a CastersApp campaign?

Not for 12 months. CastersApp's Terms forbid creators from working directly with a brand met through the platform for 12 months after first contact.[[5]](#ref-5) On Promobeez the contact you unlock is yours.

### Does CastersApp do barter?

Yes. Some open campaigns pay in tickets, meals, products or gift cards instead of cash.[[3]](#ref-3)[[4]](#ref-4) Most of its campaigns combine a cash fee with a gift.

### Do barter posts need an ad label in Finland?

Yes. A gifted meal or ticket in return for a post is commercial cooperation and must be clearly marked. See [influencer marketing disclosure in Finland](https://www.promobeez.com/blog/influencer-marketing-disclosure-finland).
