# Timeless Club alternative for local venues: Promobeez vs Timeless Club compared

By [Andrey Shepelev](https://www.promobeez.com/about/andrey-shepelev) · Published 14 Nov 2026 · Facts checked 14 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

> **Short answer.** Timeless Club is a Milan creator platform run by Timeless S.r.l. that sends selected creators to restaurants, hotels, spas and gyms (*in-person invites*) and ships products to them (*product gifting*), mostly in barter. It works like a managed club: the Timeless team vets creators, sets up each activation with you after a 30-minute demo, and publishes no prices. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace for local venues: you post an offer, nearby nano and micro creators from 500 followers apply, you unlock the ones you like and keep their contact, and Basic costs €15 a month. Pick Timeless Club if you are a brand or a high-profile venue in Italy that wants a done-for-you service and creators with tens of thousands of followers. Pick Promobeez if you run a neighbourhood café, salon or gym, want a public price and want to manage creators yourself; it is live in Helsinki and has a waiting list open in Milan.

*Disclosure: Promobeez publishes this page, so read it as a vendor comparison. Facts about Timeless Club come from thetimeless.club, its App Store listing and four Italian press articles from July 2026, all linked in the sources and checked on 14 November 2026. Timeless Club does not publish prices or its service contract, so anything about cost below is marked as not public. How we compared: we read every public page of thetimeless.club in English and Italian, its terms, its App Store listing and the July 2026 press coverage, and put its 12 published case studies into one table. We did not buy a Timeless Club plan or take the demo. Tell us if we got something wrong.*

## 2. Looking for a Timeless Club alternative? {#alternative}

Most local businesses that search for a Timeless Club alternative want one of four things Timeless Club does not offer by design: a price they can see before a sales call, a self-serve tool they can start today, small creators who live near the venue, and creator contacts they keep after the visit. Timeless Club is built the other way round: a curated club, a demo first, mid-size creators and every conversation inside its platform.[[1]](#ref-1)[[10]](#ref-10)

That is not a flaw for a fashion brand or a flagship restaurant with a marketing budget. It is a mismatch for a neighbourhood café, a hair salon or a boutique gym that wants ten locals through the door this month and wants to know the cost upfront. Promobeez is the alternative built for that second case. If you need product shipping or creators with 80k+ followers, Timeless Club or a paid platform such as [CollaborUp](https://www.promobeez.com/blog/promobeez-vs-collaborup) is the better fit.

## 3. How Timeless Club works {#timeless}

Timeless Club describes itself as "the platform connecting brands and creators" and tells creators it is "not a marketplace" but a selective club.[[1]](#ref-1)[[2]](#ref-2) The company behind it is Timeless S.r.l., registered at Via Nicolò Tartaglia 29 in Milan with €10,000 share capital.[[6]](#ref-6) Italian trade press names four founders, Roberto Oliva, Luciano Burattin, Federico Raimondi and Matteo Bortolotti, and says the first version was built in eighteen days.[[8]](#ref-8)[[9]](#ref-9)

A business works with Timeless Club in two modes. **In-person invites** cover restaurants, hotels, spas, fitness clubs and events: you define the experience, available days and slots, target, rules, dress code and brief, and Timeless Club shows the invitation only to creators who match your category, city and audience.[[3]](#ref-3) **Product gifting** ships a product to the creator, either as a 100% coupon on your e-commerce or with shipping handled by Timeless Club, which collects addresses and sizes for you.[[4]](#ref-4) Both modes start in barter. For products you can add a creator fee, agreed with the Timeless team and paid when the collaboration is complete.[[4]](#ref-4)

Applications land in a dashboard where you accept or decline each creator. The homepage shows one hard rule: unanswered applications are declined automatically four hours before arrival.[[1]](#ref-1) Every creator gets a brief with what to say, which tags to use, which formats to produce and when to publish. The first content arrives 7 to 14 days after you confirm the brief, depending on the mode, and higher tiers add audience analytics and a video compliance report.[[4]](#ref-4)

## 4. How Promobeez works {#promobeez}

Promobeez is a barter marketplace and nothing else. A business posts an offer, for example "brunch for two for a Reel and Stories". Creators with a local audience apply. Before you pay to unlock anyone, you see each applicant's engagement, how much of their audience is in your city, their content profile and how recently they posted commercial content.

You unlock the contacts you like and agree the visit directly with the creator, by message or phone. After the visit, Promobeez watches the published post so you can see that the barter was honoured. Creators never pay, and Promobeez takes no commission on the barter.[[11]](#ref-11)

There is no demo, no account manager and no shipping service. Promobeez runs in Greater Helsinki today. In Italy it has a waiting list open in Milan: Italian businesses can register now and post offers at launch.[[12]](#ref-12)

Three things stand out. First, these are mid-size creators: the median of the 12 averages is about 79k followers, and the two restaurant cases average 139k and 242k. That suits a brand that wants reach across Milan; a neighbourhood bar mostly needs people who live within a few tram stops. Second, the clients are premium names (BMW, Technogym, Molton Brown, Crazy Pizza), which matches the "club" positioning. Third, the Technogym and BMW cards show identical figures, so treat the case-study numbers as marketing, not audited data; Timeless Club's own terms say site metrics are illustrative and not a promise of results.[[6]](#ref-6)

The Crazy Pizza case also shows how reach relates to followers: 2.17M estimated reach from 78 creators is about 28k per creator, roughly a fifth of their average follower count. That ratio is normal for Stories and Reels, and a useful sanity check whatever platform you use.

The headline numbers moved fast. In July 2026 the press releases said more than 1,000 creators, more than 5,000 collaborations and more than 100 companies one year after launch.[[8]](#ref-8)[[9]](#ref-9) In November 2026 the website and App Store say 2,000+ creators and 10,000+ collaborations.[[1]](#ref-1)[[7]](#ref-7)

## 6. Timeless Club prices vs Promobeez prices {#price}

Timeless Club publishes no prices, plans, commissions or limits on any page we checked. The only route is "Book a demo", a 30-minute call booked through Google Calendar.[[1]](#ref-1)[[10]](#ref-10) The website terms say they cover only the site; the Timeless Club service runs under separate contract terms that are not public.[[6]](#ref-6) In a July 2026 interview, co-founder Luciano Burattin described a turnkey service for brands with monthly reporting on request.[[9]](#ref-9) So expect a quote shaped by your brand, volume and mode, and ask about minimum terms, cancellation and content rights before you sign.

Promobeez prices are on the website. Basic is €15 a month and your first offer includes 5 applications, all of them unlockable. After that you buy application packs: 5 for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift. There is no commission on the barter.[[11]](#ref-11)

In both cases the barter itself (the dinner, the treatment, the product) comes on top. With Timeless Club a paid creator fee can also be added on product collaborations.[[4]](#ref-4)

## 7. Who picks the creators and who talks to them {#control}

On Timeless Club, selection happens twice. The Timeless team admits creators to the club after looking at content quality, storytelling, consistency, reliability and audience.[[2]](#ref-2) Then you accept or decline the applicants for your invite or product. All communication stays inside the platform: the FAQ says creators cannot contact the business directly.[[1]](#ref-1) That protects you from cold DMs and keeps every booking in one dashboard, but you do not build your own list of creator contacts.

On Promobeez nobody pre-selects for you. Any creator from 500 followers can apply, and you decide using the numbers on each card: local audience share, engagement and recent commercial posts. Once you unlock a creator, the contact is yours, so you can invite the good ones back next season without going through the platform again.

Disclosure rules are the same on both. In Italy a free meal or product counts as payment, so under AGCOM guidelines and the IAP Digital Chart the post must say it is advertising.[[13]](#ref-13)

## 8. When Timeless Club is the better choice {#choose-timeless}

- **You want someone else to run it.** Timeless sets up the activation, briefs creators and reports back; you approve and host.
- **You ship products.** Coupons on your e-shop or managed shipping are built in; Promobeez does not ship anything.
- **You want bigger creators.** Case studies average 41k to 242k followers, and you can filter for 80k+.
- **You need Italy now.** Timeless Club is live in Milan today; Promobeez is still on a waiting list there.
- **You may pay creators.** A creator fee can be added on product collaborations.

## 9. When Promobeez is the better choice {#choose-promobeez}

- **You are a local venue** (café, restaurant, salon, gym, studio) and want people from your area to walk in.
- **You want a price before a sales call.** €15 a month is on the website; there is no demo or quote.
- **You want small local creators**, from 500 followers, chosen by how much of their audience lives in your city.
- **You want to keep the contacts** and work with the same creators again without a platform in the middle.
- **You want to test with one offer** and see how many creators apply before spending more.

## 10. Switching from Timeless Club, or using both {#switch}

The two platforms do not exclude each other. A Milan restaurant group can keep Timeless Club for launch events with larger creators and use Promobeez for the steady flow of local visits between launches. If you are moving over completely, three things make the switch easy.

- **Reuse your offer.** The experience you set up on Timeless Club (a tasting menu for two, a facial, a week of classes) becomes your Promobeez offer as it is.
- **Reuse your brief.** Tags, formats and the #adv label carry over; on Promobeez you agree them directly with each creator.
- **Check your contract first.** Timeless Club's service terms are not public, so read your own agreement for notice periods and content rights before you stop.

Promobeez is live in Helsinki and opening in Milan; join the [Milan waiting list](https://www.promobeez.com/it/per-le-attivita) to be told when you can post your first offer.

## 11. Bottom line {#bottom-line}

Timeless Club and Promobeez both turn a free experience into creator content, but they sell different things. Timeless Club sells a managed, invite-only service with mid-size creators, product shipping and a price you get after a demo. Promobeez sells a self-serve tool for local venues: €15 a month, creators from 500 followers who live nearby, and contacts you keep. If you want it done for you and reach across Italy, talk to Timeless Club. If you want locals through the door at a known cost, Promobeez is the Timeless Club alternative to try.

## 12. FAQ {#faq}

### How much does Timeless Club cost?

Timeless Club does not publish prices for businesses. You book a 30-minute demo and receive a proposal; the service contract is separate from the website terms and not public. The creator app is free to download.

### What is the best Timeless Club alternative for a local business?

For a café, restaurant, salon or gym that wants nearby creators and a public price, Promobeez is the closest alternative: barter-only, self-serve, from €15 a month. For paid campaigns with a subscription, CollaborUp is the main Italian option, with plans from €59 a month. For product seeding with managed shipping, Timeless Club itself remains the stronger tool.

### What do reviews say about Timeless Club?

The public reviews we found are from creators: the Timeless Club app has 4.9 out of 5 from 37 ratings on the Italian App Store as of November 2026. We found no published business reviews or prices, so ask Timeless Club for references from venues similar to yours.

### Is Timeless Club free for creators?

Yes, the Timeless Club app is listed as free on the App Store, and no creator fee is mentioned on the site. Creators apply to join, the Timeless team reviews their profile, and admitted creators receive invites, products and some paid collaborations.

### Who owns Timeless Club?

Timeless Club is run by Timeless S.r.l., a Milan company with VAT number 14136750966. Italian press in July 2026 named Roberto Oliva, Luciano Burattin, Federico Raimondi and Matteo Bortolotti as founders.

### Does Timeless Club work outside Italy?

Its activity and case studies are in Italy, mainly Milan, although the site is available in Italian, English, French and Spanish. In a July 2026 interview co-founder Luciano Burattin said the plan is to replicate the model in Europe.

### Is Promobeez available in Milan?

Not yet. Promobeez is live in Greater Helsinki and has a waiting list open in Milan; businesses that register now are told when they can post their first offer.
