# Invited Agency alternative for local venues: Promobeez vs Invited Agency compared

By [Andrey Shepelev](https://www.promobeez.com/about/andrey-shepelev) · Published 21 Oct 2026 · Facts checked 21 Oct 2026 · 7 min read

> **Short answer.** Invited Agency is a Helsinki influencer marketing and PR agency with its own app: you tell the team your goal, they pick creators from a network of 4,000+, run the collaboration and can host a PR event such as a restaurant opening. Price is quoted per project. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace: you post an offer (a meal, a treatment, a class), local nano and micro creators apply, and you pay only a flat plan, from €15, to unlock the contacts you want. Pick Invited when you want someone to run it for you or need an event. Pick Promobeez when you want to choose creators yourself, start with no budget and keep the relationship.

## 2. Looking for an Invited Agency alternative? {#alternative}

Most venues that search for an Invited Agency alternative are not looking for another agency. They want to stop paying for someone else's time on small, repeat collaborations. Invited prices every project as a quote after a consultation, and its team chooses the creators and runs the work.[[1]](#ref-1) That is worth paying for around an opening night. For one or two local creators a week, it is a lot of process.

Promobeez is the Invited Agency alternative for that case: a flat €15 a month, you pick creators yourself by local audience, and the contacts stay yours. If you need a PR event, content production for your own channels or simply have no time, Invited is the better fit.

## 3. How Invited Agency works {#invited}

Invited Agency describes itself as the place "where brands and influencers meet". It is based at Yrjönkatu 25 in central Helsinki and is run by founder and CEO Emilia Lepomäki with a small team that includes an account manager and an event manager.[[1]](#ref-1)

It sells three services:

- **Influencer collaborations.** You set the goal in a kickoff meeting, the team selects creators from its network and can run the whole collaboration.
- **Content production.** The team designs, shoots, writes and publishes content for your own social channels on a schedule.
- **PR events.** Invited invites influencers to openings, reopenings after renovation, new menu or product launches, seasonal campaigns, rebrands and anniversaries.

The company app (publisher VIP Guest Oy, Helsinki) lets creators browse campaigns and events by city, apply, chat with the business and track upcoming visits. Businesses can create campaigns and events, including recurring and open-date events, pick from applicants and rate influencers afterwards.[[2]](#ref-2) Creators have no fixed follower minimum; the team looks at profile and content fit.[[1]](#ref-1)

Its client list leans towards Helsinki hospitality and leisure: Ravintola Teatteri, Mall of Tripla, Allas Pool, Gillet Bar & Bistro, Burgers & Wine, Muru, IZZA Palapizza, Eckerö Line and others.[[1]](#ref-1)

## 4. How Promobeez works {#promobeez}

Promobeez is a barter marketplace. A business posts an offer, for example "dinner for two for a Reel and three Stories". Creators with a local audience apply. Before you pay anything, you see a structured view of each applicant: engagement, the share of their audience that is local, content profile and how recently they posted commercial content. Creator identity stays hidden until you unlock.

When you like a match, you unlock the contact and agree the visit directly, usually on WhatsApp or Instagram. The relationship is yours. After the visit, Promobeez watches the published post, so you can see whether the barter was honoured. Creators never pay.[[3]](#ref-3)

## 5. Invited Agency prices vs Promobeez: quote vs flat plan {#price}

Invited Agency does not publish prices. Its FAQ says pricing is tailored to the company's goals and the scope of the campaign, and it offers a free consultation.[[1]](#ref-1) That is normal for an agency: you pay for the team's time, the selection and the event work.

Promobeez prices are public:[[3]](#ref-3)

- **Basic, €15/month:** offers, creator cards, CRM, meeting calendar and post tracking, 5 creator checks a month. No fee on collaborations while you are subscribed.
- **Welcome bonus:** your first offer includes 5 applications, and you can contact all five.
- **Application packs:** 5 applications for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift. You pay only for applications that arrive.

The campaign itself costs what your offer costs you: the food cost of a dinner, a haircut, a day pass.

For scale: Finnish agencies that publish prices quote €5,000 to €50,000 per influencer campaign.[[4]](#ref-4) We have no evidence that Invited charges in that range; we only note that a quoted agency project and a €15 software plan are different purchases.

## 6. When Invited Agency is the better choice {#choose-invited}

Be honest with yourself about time. Invited is the better fit when:

- **You are opening, reopening or launching a menu** and want a room full of creators on one evening. Promobeez does not organise events.
- **Nobody on your team has time** to read applications, message creators and book visits.
- **You also want someone to run your own Instagram or TikTok.** Invited sells content production; Promobeez does not.
- **You want one partner to be accountable** for the result, with a kickoff meeting and a named account manager.

## 7. When Promobeez is the better choice {#choose-promobeez}

Promobeez is the better fit when:

- **You want to test on a small budget.** Basic is €15 a month and your first offer includes 5 applications, so you see who applies before you buy more.
- **You want a known monthly cost**, not a quote.
- **You want to pick creators yourself**, by how much of their audience lives near you, not only by follower count.
- **You want to keep the creators.** Contacts you unlock stay yours; a good creator can come back next season without a middleman.
- **You run steady, small collabs** (one or two creators a week) rather than one big moment.

## 8. Switching from Invited Agency, or using both {#switch}

For some venues using both makes sense. A restaurant can use an agency for the opening night and Promobeez for the months after, when the goal is a steady flow of local posts on quiet weekdays. The tools solve different problems: one buys an event and someone else's time, the other gives you a self-serve channel. If you move your regular collaborations over:

- **Turn your brief into an offer.** "Dinner for two for a Reel and three Stories" is a complete Promobeez offer.
- **Set aside 20 minutes a week.** You read applications and message creators yourself; that is the trade-off for the lower cost.
- **Keep the agency for events.** Promobeez does not organise openings or launches.

Start with [Promobeez Basic for €15 a month](https://www.promobeez.com/for-businesses) and see which local creators apply.

## 9. Bottom line {#bottom-line}

Invited Agency is a Helsinki agency with an app: it selects creators from its network, runs collaborations and PR events, and quotes each project. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace: €15 a month, you choose local creators yourself and keep their contacts. Choose Invited when you want an event or someone else to do the work. Choose Promobeez when you want steady local posts at a known cost.

## 10. FAQ {#faq}

### Is Invited Agency an app or an agency?

Both. It is a Helsinki influencer marketing and PR agency, and it runs an app (Invited Agency, by VIP Guest Oy) where creators apply to campaigns and events and businesses manage them.[[1]](#ref-1)[[2]](#ref-2)

### How much does Invited Agency cost?

It is not published. Invited says pricing is tailored to goals and scope and offers a free consultation.[[1]](#ref-1)

### How much does Promobeez cost?

Basic is €15 a month, and your first offer includes 5 applications you can all contact. Extra applications come in packs: 5 for €34, or 12 for €68 with 2 as a gift. You pay only for applications that arrive. Creators always join free.[[3]](#ref-3)

### What is the best Invited Agency alternative for a local business?

If you want to run creator collaborations yourself at a fixed low cost, Promobeez is the closest alternative, from €15 a month. If you want paid campaigns on a public price list, Vaiku starts at €149 a month. If you want another managed service with UGC videos, look at CastersApp.

### What do reviews say about Invited Agency?

The Invited Agency app has 4.1 out of 5 from 9 ratings on the Finnish App Store as of November 2026; it is used by both creators and businesses. Invited lists Helsinki clients such as Ravintola Teatteri, Mall of Tripla and Allas Pool on its site.

### Do creators get paid on Promobeez?

The standard deal is barter: a meal, service, product or experience in return for content. If you want to add a fee, you agree it with the creator directly.

### Which is better for a small restaurant in Helsinki?

If you need an opening event or have no time, an agency like Invited. If you want ongoing local posts at a fixed low cost and want to choose the creators, Promobeez. Many venues start with Promobeez Basic at €15 to see how many local creators want their offer before they buy more.

### Do I need to mark barter posts as advertising in Finland?

Yes. A gifted meal in return for a post is commercial cooperation under Finnish rules, and the post must say so clearly. See our guide to [influencer marketing disclosure in Finland](https://www.promobeez.com/blog/influencer-marketing-disclosure-finland).
