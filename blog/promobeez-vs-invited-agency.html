Promobeez vs Invited Agency: which one fits a local business in Finland?

Short answerInvited Agency is a Helsinki influencer marketing and PR agency with its own app: you tell the team your goal, they pick creators from a network of 4,000+, run the collaboration and can host a PR event such as a restaurant opening. Price is quoted per project. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace: you post an offer (a meal, a treatment, a class), local nano and micro creators apply, and you pay only a flat plan, from €15, to unlock the contacts you want. Pick Invited when you want someone to run it for you or need an event. Pick Promobeez when you want to choose creators yourself, start with no budget and keep the relationship.

2. How Invited Agency works

Invited Agency describes itself as the place "where brands and influencers meet". It is based at Yrjönkatu 25 in central Helsinki and is run by founder and CEO Emilia Lepomäki with a small team that includes an account manager and an event manager.[1]

It sells three services:

  • Influencer collaborations. You set the goal in a kickoff meeting, the team selects creators from its network and can run the whole collaboration.
  • Content production. The team designs, shoots, writes and publishes content for your own social channels on a schedule.
  • PR events. Invited invites influencers to openings, reopenings after renovation, new menu or product launches, seasonal campaigns, rebrands and anniversaries.

The company app (publisher VIP Guest Oy, Helsinki) lets creators browse campaigns and events by city, apply, chat with the business and track upcoming visits. Businesses can create campaigns and events, including recurring and open-date events, pick from applicants and rate influencers afterwards.[2] Creators have no fixed follower minimum; the team looks at profile and content fit.[1]

Its client list leans towards Helsinki hospitality and leisure: Ravintola Teatteri, Mall of Tripla, Allas Pool, Gillet Bar & Bistro, Burgers & Wine, Muru, IZZA Palapizza, Eckerö Line and others.[1]

3. How Promobeez works

Promobeez is a barter marketplace. A business posts an offer, for example "dinner for two for a Reel and three Stories". Creators with a local audience apply. Before you pay anything, you see a structured view of each applicant: engagement, the share of their audience that is local, content profile and how recently they posted commercial content. Creator identity stays hidden until you unlock.

When you like a match, you unlock the contact and agree the visit directly, usually on WhatsApp or Instagram. The relationship is yours. After the visit, Promobeez watches the published post, so you can see whether the barter was honoured. Creators never pay.[3]

4. Price: quote vs flat plan

Invited Agency does not publish prices. Its FAQ says pricing is tailored to the company's goals and the scope of the campaign, and it offers a free consultation.[1] That is normal for an agency: you pay for the team's time, the selection and the event work.

Promobeez prices are public:[3]

  • (almost) Free: €15 once for 1 offer in total and 3 contact unlocks, refunded in one click if fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days.
  • Starter, €69/month: 1 active offer, 10 unlocks a month.
  • Growth, €109/month: unlimited offers and unlocks, featured placement in the creator feed.
  • New businesses get 14 days of Growth free.

The campaign itself costs what your offer costs you: the food cost of a dinner, a haircut, a day pass.

For scale: Finnish agencies that publish prices quote €5,000 to €50,000 per influencer campaign.[4] We have no evidence that Invited charges in that range; we only note that a quoted agency project and a €69 software plan are different purchases.

5. When Invited Agency is the better choice

Be honest with yourself about time. Invited is the better fit when:

  • You are opening, reopening or launching a menu and want a room full of creators on one evening. Promobeez does not organise events.
  • Nobody on your team has time to read applications, message creators and book visits.
  • You also want someone to run your own Instagram or TikTok. Invited sells content production; Promobeez does not.
  • You want one partner to be accountable for the result, with a kickoff meeting and a named account manager.

6. When Promobeez is the better choice

Promobeez is the better fit when:

  • You want to test before you spend. (almost) Free lets you post one offer for €15 and see who applies. If fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days, you get the €15 back.
  • You want a known monthly cost, not a quote.
  • You want to pick creators yourself, by how much of their audience lives near you, not only by follower count.
  • You want to keep the creators. Contacts you unlock stay yours; a good creator can come back next season without a middleman.
  • You run steady, small collabs (one or two creators a week) rather than one big moment.

7. Can you use both?

Yes, and for some venues it makes sense. A restaurant can use an agency for the opening night and Promobeez for the months after, when the goal is a steady flow of local posts on quiet weekdays. The tools solve different problems: one buys an event and someone else's time, the other gives you a self-serve channel.

8. FAQ

Is Invited Agency an app or an agency?

Both. It is a Helsinki influencer marketing and PR agency, and it runs an app (Invited Agency, by VIP Guest Oy) where creators apply to campaigns and events and businesses manage them.[1][2]

How much does Invited Agency cost?

It is not published. Invited says pricing is tailored to goals and scope and offers a free consultation.[1]

How much does Promobeez cost?

€15 once for one offer with 3 unlocks (refunded in one click if fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days), then €69 or €109 a month. Creators always join free.[3]

Do creators get paid on Promobeez?

The standard deal is barter: a meal, service, product or experience in return for content. If you want to add a fee, you agree it with the creator directly.

Which is better for a small restaurant in Helsinki?

If you need an opening event or have no time, an agency like Invited. If you want ongoing local posts at a fixed low cost and want to choose the creators, Promobeez. Many venues start with Promobeez (almost) Free to see how many local creators want their offer before they buy anything.

Do I need to mark barter posts as advertising in Finland?

Yes. A gifted meal in return for a post is commercial cooperation under Finnish rules, and the post must say so clearly. See our guide to influencer marketing disclosure in Finland.
