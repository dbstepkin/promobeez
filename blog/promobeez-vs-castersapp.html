Promobeez vs CastersApp: which creator platform fits a local business in Finland?

CastersApp runs managed UGC and influencer campaigns with a campaign manager. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace for local venues from €15. Model, cost, rules and fit compared, checked October 2026.

Short answerCastersApp, from the Finnish agency Casters, is a managed creator marketplace: you book a demo, a campaign manager proposes creators, both you and Casters approve every post, and all payments run through the platform. It is strong on UGC videos for your ads and on paid influencer posts. Prices for brands are not published. Promobeez is a self-serve barter marketplace for local venues: you post an offer such as a dinner or a treatment, nearby nano and micro creators apply, you pay a flat plan from €15 to €109 a month to unlock the contacts you choose, and the creator relationship is yours to keep. Pick CastersApp if you want UGC for ads or a managed paid campaign. Pick Promobeez if you want steady local visits for the cost of what you already sell.

Disclosure: Promobeez publishes this page, so read it as a vendor comparison. Every fact about CastersApp comes from castersapp.com (home, business page, creator page, open campaigns and Terms of Service), read on 22 October 2026. If something has changed, tell us and we will fix it.

2. How CastersApp works

Casters started as an influencer agency in Helsinki and built CastersApp as its platform. The business page says the team handles "creators, briefing, coordination and reporting" for you, and the homepage promises each brand its own campaign manager from start to finish.[1][2]

The process has five steps: you state the goal, Casters suggests creators, you approve them, the creators produce the content, and you get a report.[2] Creators see open campaigns in the app and apply. On 22 October 2026 there were 21 open campaigns, across four formats:[3]

  • UGC videos that the brand posts on its own Meta and TikTok accounts. Example: a supplement brand paying €170 per selected video plus €20 per extra hook.
  • Influencer posts on the creator's own account. Example: a Helsinki restaurant offering €100, a €100 gift card and a meal for two for one Reel and three Stories.
  • Barter. Example: a Christmas party event offering tickets for the creator and friends in return for a pre-event post and Stories.
  • Events and product seeding.

Creators join free, need more than 1,000 followers, and see the reward before they apply.[4] The site gives two sets of totals: 2,000+ influencers, 50+ brands and 150+ campaigns on the homepage, and 1,000+ creators, 100+ Finnish brands and 500+ campaigns on the business page.[1][2]

CastersApp lists restaurants as a target group and shows a restaurant launch case with 10 food creators, 40+ pieces of content and table bookings up 200%. The restaurant is not named.[2]

3. How Promobeez works

Promobeez is a barter marketplace. A business posts an offer, for example "dinner for two for a Reel and two Stories". Creators with a local audience apply. Before you pay anything, you see each applicant's engagement, how much of their audience is local, their content profile and how recently they did commercial posts. Identity stays hidden until you unlock.

You unlock the contacts you like and agree the visit directly. After the visit, Promobeez watches the published post so you can see that the barter was honoured. We hold no money. Creators never pay.[6]

4. What a restaurant collab costs on each

CastersApp does not publish business pricing. Its business FAQ calls pricing "transparent", but the pricing link leads to the FAQ, not to a price list, and the main call to action is "book a demo".[2] What we can see is the creator side. In the restaurant campaign above, each creator receives €100 in cash, a €100 gift card and a meal for two.[3] Casters' own FAQ puts micro-influencers (1,000 to 10,000 followers) at €50 to €500 per post.[2] Whatever Casters charges the brand comes on top.

On Promobeez the cost is the plan plus your offer:[6]

  • (almost) Free: €15 once for 1 offer in total and 3 contact unlocks, refunded in one click if fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days.
  • Starter, €69/month: 1 active offer, 10 unlocks a month.
  • Growth, €109/month: unlimited offers and unlocks.
  • New businesses get 14 days of Growth free.

If a dinner for two costs you €25 in food, that is the campaign. A cash fee does buy more control: fixed deliverables, deadlines and, on CastersApp, a formal approval step and usage rights.

5. The rules creators sign

The CastersApp Terms of Service (updated January 2026) set strict rules for creators, and they shape what you get as a brand:[5]

  • Nothing is posted until both the brand and Casters approve it, and creators must revise until both are satisfied, with no cap on rounds.
  • Posts must stay live for six months. No other ads 12 hours before or after, and no direct competitor for one week.
  • The brand may repost the content on its own organic channels for the campaign plus six months. Using it in paid ads needs a separate content licence fee.
  • Payments are held by CastersApp and released after the post is verified and a 14-day window.
  • Non-circumvention: for 12 months after first contact, a creator may not work directly with a brand met through CastersApp. Breaking this closes the account and forfeits pending pay.

For a brand that wants control, these rules are a plus. For a café that wants the same three local creators back every month on its own terms, the non-circumvention clause matters: the relationship stays on the platform for a year.

Promobeez works the other way round. Once you unlock a contact, you can invite that creator back whenever you like, directly.

6. When CastersApp is the better choice

  • You want UGC videos for your own ads, with formal usage rights. Promobeez does not sell UGC.
  • You want someone to run it: a campaign manager, a creator shortlist and a report.
  • You pay creators cash and want contracts, approvals and payouts handled.
  • You need content from outside your neighbourhood, or a campaign for an event, a car or a cruise.
  • You want a formal approval step before anything goes live.

7. When Promobeez is the better choice

  • You are a local venue (café, restaurant, salon, gym, studio) and want people from your area to visit.
  • You want a known price and can start at €15, refunded in one click if fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days.
  • You would rather give a meal than pay a fee.
  • You want to choose creators yourself, by local audience, not from a shortlist.
  • You want to keep the creator contact and work with good creators again without a middleman.

8. FAQ

What is CastersApp?

A Finnish creator marketplace run by the influencer agency Casters. Brands get UGC videos, influencer posts, barter and event campaigns with a campaign manager. Creators join free from 1,000 followers.[1][4]

How much does CastersApp cost for a business?

Business prices are not published; you book a demo. On the creator side, open campaigns pay from about €100 per post or video plus gifts, and Casters' FAQ quotes €50 to €500 per post for micro-influencers.[2][3]

How much does Promobeez cost?

€15 once for one offer with 3 unlocks (refunded in one click if fewer than 3 creators apply in 14 days), then Starter €69 or Growth €109 a month. New businesses get 14 days of Growth free.[6]

Can I work with a creator directly after a CastersApp campaign?

Not for 12 months. CastersApp's Terms forbid creators from working directly with a brand met through the platform for 12 months after first contact.[5] On Promobeez the contact you unlock is yours.

Does CastersApp do barter?

Yes. Some open campaigns pay in tickets, meals, products or gift cards instead of cash.[3][4] Most of its campaigns combine a cash fee with a gift.

Do barter posts need an ad label in Finland?

Yes. A gifted meal or ticket in return for a post is commercial cooperation and must be clearly marked. See influencer marketing disclosure in Finland.
